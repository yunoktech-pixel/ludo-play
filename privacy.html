This Privacy Policy explains how the Ludo Tokens mobile game for Android ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. We built Ludo Tokens to work offline and to keep as little information as possible.

1. Summary

  • No account, sign-up or login is needed.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your settings and any unfinished match are stored only on your device.
  • We do not run our own servers for the App, and we do not sell personal information.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device so the game works as expected:

  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, auto-move and animation speed preferences, the last game mode you chose, and whether you have seen the first-game tip.
  • Game progress: your unfinished match (board position, players and turn) so you can continue later, and a count of matches you have completed.

This information never leaves your device through the App, and we cannot access it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Ludo Tokens → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads (a banner on the home screen and occasional full-screen ads between matches), provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, personalise (where permitted), measure and protect ads against fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

Ads need an internet connection. When you play offline, no ads are requested and no ad data is sent.

4. Consent and your ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are requested, the App shows Google's consent form so you can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California): where required, Google's consent tools let you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

If you refuse personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on general context rather than your profile.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads. Gameplay does not need the internet.
  • Vibrate: used for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for the purposes described above.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

6. Children

Ludo Tokens is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies. Because we do not hold any of your data on our own servers, there is no account data for us to delete. For Google's ad data, use the controls in section 4 or My Ad Center.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to withdraw consent and to object to processing. Since the App keeps your game data only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly as described above. For anything else, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Game data stays in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Ad traffic is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit risk by not collecting personal information ourselves.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the App changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Please review it periodically.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, email us at connecttodroidstack@gmail.com.
